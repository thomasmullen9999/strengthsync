Tips

A selection of tips to help you get in shape and stay consistent.

  • 🥩

    When attempting to build muscle, try and get around 1.2-2.0 grams of protein per kg of body weight per day.

  • 🥑

    Don't be scared of eating a healthy amount of fats; this macronutrient is an important and required part of your diet.

  • 🏋️

    When strength training, try to ensure you are lifting with good form before adding more weight to the bar or machine.

  • 📈

    Try to eat around 250-500 calories more than your maintenance calories when bulking.
